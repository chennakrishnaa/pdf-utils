OFFKIT PRIVACY POLICY
=====================

Effective date: 23 November 2026

Offkit is an offline PDF and image toolkit for Android. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: no data collected. Works offline.

WHAT WE COLLECT
---------------

Offkit collects no personal data. It does not store your data on any server, and it does not share or sell personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no trackers.

PERMISSIONS AND NETWORK
-----------------------

The app requests no Android permissions and makes no network connections.

YOUR FILES
----------

You choose files through the system file picker or photo picker. They are processed entirely on your device.

While a job runs, the app keeps temporary working copies in its private cache. They are deleted after each job (when the result is saved or discarded, or the job fails or is cancelled). Any copies left behind, for example if the app was closed mid-job, are deleted the next time a tool is opened after the app starts.

The result is written only to the place you choose in the save dialog. If you choose a cloud storage location, that storage app may upload the file; Offkit itself never sends it anywhere.

RECENT FILES
------------

The Recent files list on the home screen shows files you saved with the app. For each one it keeps the file name, size, the time it was saved, which tool made it, and a link to the file so the app can open it again. This list is stored only on your device and is excluded from backup. It is removed when you tap Clear, when you clear the app's data, or when you uninstall the app. Clearing the list does not delete the files themselves.

If you open or share a file from the list, Android hands it to another app on your device.

The app also remembers on your device that you have seen the introduction and which cards you have hidden; these are simple settings, contain nothing about you or your files, are excluded from backup, and are removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling.

PHOTO METADATA
--------------

When the app places images into a PDF, it removes photo metadata such as location and camera details. Unusual or damaged image files may still keep some embedded metadata. Colour-profile data inside photos (which can name the camera or device model) is kept, because it is needed to show colours correctly. A PDF you give to Compress or Add images keeps its own document properties, such as author, title and other embedded metadata. Metadata inside the pages of PDFs you give to Merge may also remain in the result.

LINKS
-----

Tapping the source code link or the privacy policy link in About opens it in your browser, or in another app that handles web links. That app is outside Offkit, and its own privacy terms apply. The same goes for the Support page: opening a link, email or share sheet hands over to another app of your choice, and Offkit itself sends nothing.

CHILDREN
--------

The app is not directed at children. It collects nothing from anyone, children included.
